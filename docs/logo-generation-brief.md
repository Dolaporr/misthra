# Misthra — logo generation brief

Use this document with an image-generation or brand-design AI. Begin with the master prompt below. The goal is an original, usable identity; this is a design brief, not a claim of trademark clearance.

## The company

**Name:** Misthra

**Descriptor:** The agent work exchange on Elysium.

**Tagline:** Good work. Earned rewards.

Misthra is a marketplace where people post paid missions, find human specialists or AI agents, review deliverables and reward accepted work in HYPE. It brings a clear brief, a capable worker and an agreed reward together. The product currently runs as a local preview with simulated rewards; the logo must not imply that payments or a public launch are already live.

The name is inspired by Greek *misthos*, associated with pay or reward. Misthra is our brand spelling, not a literal Greek translation. Elysium is the intended ecosystem, not the company name. Misthra is an independent product; do not imply official Elysium, Kinetiq or Hyperliquid ownership.

## Brand character

Precise, capable, composed and quietly futuristic. A place for useful work and dependable exchange. It should feel distinctive enough for an ambitious technology company and clear enough for a person earning their first reward.

The existing website uses charcoal, silver, blue, smoky mirror glass and reflective android imagery. The logo should sit naturally in that world while remaining recognizable in plain black or white. Reflective chrome is a presentation treatment, not something required for the symbol to make sense.

## Master prompt — paste this first

> Act as a senior identity designer. Design an original logo for **Misthra**, a marketplace connecting people and AI agents to complete paid missions. Its tagline is **Good work. Earned rewards.** The core idea is a clear brief meeting the right skill, followed by an earned reward.
>
> Explore three genuinely different logo concepts, each shown as a symbol with the exact wordmark **misthra**. Keep typography and symbols on a neutral flat background so I can judge the identity. Give each concept a short explanation of the idea and how it remains recognizable at a small size.
>
> Concept A: an original M built from two paths that meet. Suggest commissioner and agent coming together through a confident, continuous gesture. Give the outline an intentional rhythm and a memorable silhouette. Avoid making an ordinary typed M the entire concept.
>
> Concept B: a compact abstract connection mark. Explore two complementary forms meeting around a deliberate opening or negative space. Convey cooperation and an exchange of value without a literal handshake, chain link or currency sign.
>
> Concept C: your strongest independent idea based on useful work, agreement and earned progress. It must have a different silhouette and construction from A and B. Avoid generic technology symbols.
>
> The aesthetic is sophisticated, restrained and slightly futuristic, compatible with a dark chrome and mirror-glass website. Develop the underlying identity in one solid colour first. Use silver-white **#EEF1F7** on charcoal **#0C0F16**, and also show black on white. Blue **#7FA8FF** or **#315BD5** may be an optional accent, but colour must not carry the recognition.
>
> Create custom or carefully adjusted lettering with excellent spacing and the exact spelling **misthra**. It should be readable and calm, with one or two distinctive letter details. The symbol and wordmark should work together without competing. Do not add the tagline to the small primary lockup.
>
> Avoid robots, android faces, human hands, brains, circuit boards, sparkles, lightning bolts, hexagonal badges, cubes, rockets, shields, coins, Greek columns, laurel wreaths and generic AI swooshes. Do not copy Nokia, Meta, OpenAI, Elysium, Hyperliquid or any other brand. Do not present lettering as a gradient or hide weak geometry behind a metallic effect.
>
> Show simple flat artwork first. Do not begin with a business-card mockup, 3D scene, website screenshot or wall sign. Keep the mark bold enough for a favicon, with no hairline details or tiny enclosed gaps. Prefer a few purposeful curves and joins over decorative complexity.

## Refinement prompt — after choosing one concept

> Refine only the selected Misthra concept. Preserve its defining silhouette and idea. Improve optical balance, line weight, spacing and the relationship between symbol and wordmark. Show a horizontal lockup, symbol alone, wordmark alone and a stacked lockup. Show light-on-dark and dark-on-light versions. Include a real small-size preview so I can judge it as a 24px navigation mark and a 16px favicon. Remove any detail that disappears at those sizes. Keep the spelling exactly **misthra**.

## Optional chrome presentation — only after the flat logo works

> Use the approved flat Misthra logo without changing its geometry. Create a secondary presentation render in polished silver chrome, with restrained blue reflections and controlled studio lighting against charcoal **#0C0F16**. Keep the silhouette clean and recognizable. No neon haze, excessive lens flare or busy background. This is a brand image; the primary logo remains the flat version.

## What to ask the designer or AI for

- Primary horizontal symbol-and-wordmark lockup.
- Standalone symbol, standalone wordmark and stacked lockup.
- Single-colour white, black and optional blue variants.
- Transparent-background PNG exports with generous clear space.
- An editable vector master, preferably SVG, from a tool that actually supports vector output. A raster image saved with an SVG extension is not a vector logo.
- A small usage sheet recording colours, minimum size and clear space.
- The optional chrome render as a separate secondary asset.

Image AIs often distort lettering and cannot reliably deliver clean vector paths. Use their output to choose and refine the concept, then check spelling and rebuild the approved geometry in a vector editor if needed.

## How we will choose

The strongest option should pass these checks:

1. Recognizable as a silhouette, without chrome, animation or blue.
2. Legible at navigation and favicon sizes.
3. Distinct from familiar technology logos.
4. The wordmark reads **misthra** immediately.
5. Feels appropriate for both human workers and AI agents.
6. Looks convincing on our dark glass interface and on a plain document.

Choose the concept with the strongest simple shape, then refine it. A dramatic mockup alone is not the identity.
